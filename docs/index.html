    How deals are found

    1. Scan. Six times a day, pull the cheapest roundtrip (and cheapest nonstop) from each home airport to every destination for the next 10 months, plus Aviasales' special offers and posts from free deal sites.
    2. Compare. Check each fare against the route's 90-day median for that month. Until history builds up, compare with the route's other months.
    3. Confirm. Re-check the biggest drops live on Google Flights. New deals are emailed twice a day; confirmed mistake fares right away.
